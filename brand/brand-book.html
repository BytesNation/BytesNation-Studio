01

Logo

Three nodes on a spine, two links, one live node. The accent node is the only color in the mark.

The lockup

The wordmark is the name in Inter Tight, weight 500, tracked at -0.04em. In the site header, at 17px, it is weight 600 and tracked at -0.03em so it holds beside a 26px mark.

The parts

The icon tile carries a simpler mark: two nodes, the spine, and the live node. The middle node and its stub drop out, so the shape still reads at 16px. The tile and the avatar are always dark, because dark is the default ground.

Clear space and size

Keep one unit clear on every side. The gap between the mark and the wordmark is the same unit.

Do not

  • Add a glow, halo, shadow or gradient to any part.
  • Recolor the live node, or color any other node.
  • Set the wordmark in the accent, or in two colors.
  • Set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or redraw the mark.
  • Place it on a photograph or a busy ground.
  • Put it inside a pill, a badge or a rounded card.
  • Write the name as two words, or without the s.

06

Motion

Springs from the Motion library, named by the job they do. Only transform and opacity move.

Six springs

press

160 ms, bounce 0

Hover, press, toggles.

snap

260 ms, bounce 0.14

Markers that slide: nav, tabs, table of contents.

settle

420 ms, bounce 0.24

Things that land: the menu, the mesh after a drag, shared elements.

layout

380 ms, bounce 0.1

List items changing place when a filter changes.

reveal

550 ms, bounce 0

Scroll reveals. No overshoot, so text never wobbles.

entrance

720 ms, bounce 0

The first-paint entrance of a page header.

Rules

  • Animate transform and opacity. Never a layout property.
  • The page title never animates. It is the largest paint and arrives with the first frame.
  • First-paint entrances are CSS, staggered, and need no script.
  • A group of tabs or links shares one accent bar, and the bar slides.
  • Every page is complete with reduced motion on and with JavaScript off.

07

Signal Mesh

The brand's one illustration: a sphere of network nodes and links.

On the homepage it rotates slowly, takes a drag, and unfolds into a rack as the page scrolls. This still is its first frame, and what a visitor keeps with motion off or scripts off.

  • Monochrome: nodes in the text color, links in muted text, the ring in the hairline color.
  • Exactly one accent node at a time.
  • No glow, bloom, gradient or heads-up-display dressing.
  • 88 nodes on a desktop, 64 on a tablet, 44 on a phone.
  • Any readout beside it is illustrative, and says so.

08

Voice

One builder writing down what happened. These lines are on the site today.

How it is written

  • I for the builder, we for the lab. Field notes and the About page are first person singular. Product, privacy and sign-up copy speaks for the lab.
  • Short sentences. Periods do the work. A headline can be two of them.
  • Plain English first. The technical detail follows for the reader who wants it.
  • The failure is part of the note. What broke and why goes in beside the fix.
  • Numbers over adjectives. A count, a version, a measured time.
  • No em dashes. Commas, colons, semicolons and parentheses.
  • Names are one word. BytesNation. MokeyBytes.

10

Component sourcing

Where a section may come from depends on whether the repository is private or public.

Private repositories

May start from Shadcnblocks

A section can begin as a Shadcnblocks block and is then restyled to this book. The repository stays private, the registry key stays in a gitignored file, and every file that began as a block is listed in the repository's ledger.

Public repositories

Never contain Shadcnblocks

No block code, no registry entry, nothing ported from a block. They build from this kit: the tokens, the bn primitives, the springs and the mark, with shadcn/ui from its own open registry or plain HTML where behavior is needed.

A repository that has held block code does not become public. An open-source project, or a fork of one, is public tier whatever its visibility, and block code never shares a program with copyleft code. The method, the licence reasons and the guard script that checks both tiers are in component-sourcing.md, beside this file.
